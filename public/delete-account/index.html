Delete your Lethal Lifts account

You can permanently delete your Lethal Lifts account and data at any time. You don't need to install anything.

How to delete it

  1. Sign in at www.lethallifts.com.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the Hub.
  3. Tap Delete Account under your email address.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

What gets deleted

Your login, profile, workouts, programs, custom exercises, weight entries, weight goals and progress photos are deleted right away and can't be recovered.

Subscriptions

If you have Lethal Lifts Pro, deleting your account cancels your subscription immediately. Any remaining paid time is not refunded automatically, so you may want to wait until the end of your billing period.

What we keep

Payment records stay with Stripe for as long as tax and accounting rules require. See our Privacy Policy for more.

Can't sign in?

Email cheflethallifts@gmail.com from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you within 30 days.
