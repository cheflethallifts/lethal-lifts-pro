Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026 (updated to add in-app account deletion)

Lethal Lifts is a workout tracking app available at www.lethallifts.com. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address. If you sign in with Google, we also receive the name, email address and profile picture on your Google account.
  • Training data: the programs, custom exercises, workout sessions, sets, weights and reps you enter.
  • Body data: the body weight entries, weight goals and progress photos you choose to add.
  • Subscription information: your Pro status and Stripe customer ID. Stripe collects your payment details directly. We never receive or store your full card number.
  • Usage data: basic analytics about page visits and app performance, collected through Vercel Analytics.
  • Data on your device: the app stores preferences, in-progress sessions and unsynced workouts in your browser so it can keep working when you lose signal.

How we use your information

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To store your training and body data and show it back to you, including charts and the muscle heat map.
  • To process subscriptions and unlock Pro features.
  • To send the login codes you request.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.

Google user data

When you choose Sign in with Google, we use your Google name, email address and profile picture only to create your account, sign you in and identify you in the app. We store this information with your account. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train artificial intelligence models. We share it only with the service providers listed below, and only so they can run the app for us.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the providers that help us run Lethal Lifts:

  • Supabase: account sign-in, database and photo storage.
  • Stripe: payments and subscriptions.
  • Vercel: website hosting and analytics.
  • Google: Sign in with Google, if you use it.
  • Our email delivery provider: sending login codes.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How we store and protect it

Your account data is stored with our service providers and is accessed over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account in the app, we immediately delete your account, training data, body data and progress photos. If you can't sign in and ask us by email instead, we delete them within 30 days. Stripe and we may keep payment records for as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete workouts, weight entries and goals in the app.
  • You can delete your account at any time from the bottom of the Hub.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data or a correction by emailing us at the address below.
  • You can revoke Lethal Lifts' access to your Google account at any time in your Google account settings.

Children

Lethal Lifts is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.

Contact us

Email cheflethallifts@gmail.com with any questions or requests about your privacy.
